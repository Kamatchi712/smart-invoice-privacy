Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

About the Application

Smart Invoice Extractor helps users extract information from invoices and organize it in Google Sheets.

Google Account and Permissions

Users may sign in with their Google account. The application requests Google permissions needed for the features the user chooses to use.

Data Use

Invoice information is processed to provide the application's extraction and spreadsheet features. Google account information is used for authentication and to identify the signed-in user.

Data Sharing and Storage

Data handling depends on the application's configuration and the Google services the user authorizes. The application does not claim that data is never shared or stored by third-party services.

Security and Contact

Users should avoid uploading confidential information unless they understand how it is processed. For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the contact details provided on the application website.
